  ## Observations

<h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Time and Labor Management</h3>

Creating traffic information panels for traffic marking and traffic safety projects is a time-consuming process. Since the process is iterative, it requires workforce. The project changes multiple times based on the decisions of the General Directorate of Highways Traffic Branch (During project delivery, there are usually three or four major changes, and the work always starts over from the beginning!).

  ### The Lack of Widely Used Software and Current Technology Products

There is no software in Turkey that creates traffic information panel drawings in DWG or DGN format. Traffic information panels are mostly drawn manually by technical operators or engineers in MicroStation or AutoCAD environments. The TrueType font is the most common font format for operating systems and is cross-platform compatible. However, there are no TrueType font definitions available for traffic information panels in Turkey.

  ### Production Errors

Each company and/or engineer uses different and non-standardized cells for fonts and elements in traffic panels. Engineers often resort to their primitive archives for each project, and due to the urgency of the projects, they frequently fail to notice the errors in their archives. As a result, the same mistakes can be found in every project over the years.

  ### Implementation Errors

In Turkey, there are standards for traffic information panels, but non-standard panels, especially in municipal areas, are easily noticeable by everyone. This situation is largely due to the lack of software that integrates standards and technical knowledge.

  ## Achievements with TRSign

This software, capable of creating traffic information panels for traffic marking and traffic safety projects in Turkey, has the following features:

 - Written in current programming languages, expandable for future needs,
 - Includes standardized digital libraries and specifications,
 - Contains TrueType fonts defined by Turkish Standards,
 - Has a user-friendly and easily accessible interface,
 - Easily editable in ASCII format and has a small file size, enabling the following:
	- Storing files on servers as needed,
	- Cross-platform use,
	- Efficient storage and sharing of data rather than the drawing itself.
- Includes a library creator for panel files generated by this software,
- Contains not only Turkish Standards but also project experiences,
- Produces panels that allow efficient conversion between drawing formats (dgn-dwg).
